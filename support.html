Support

How can we help?

If something's not working, or you have a question about an order, here's the fastest way to reach us.

In the app Open ChinaRa, go to Profile → Contact Support, and send us a message directly. This is the quickest way to get help, especially for order-specific questions.
By email Prefer email, or don't have the app installed yet? Reach us at projecteverything045@gmail.com.

Frequently asked questions

How does ordering work?

Send us a screenshot or product link of something you want. We'll send back a quote, you confirm and pay in the app, and we purchase and pack the item. Once it ships, you can track it all the way from China to Tanzania.

How do I pay?

Once you confirm your order in the app, you can pay directly by bank transfer or mobile money.

How do I track my order?

Your order status updates automatically in the app as it moves through confirmation, purchase, shipping, and arrival in Tanzania.

I have a different issue not covered here

No problem — message us through Profile → Contact Support in the app, or email us, and we'll get back to you.
